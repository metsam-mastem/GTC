# GTC Universal Device Taxonomy

Taxonomy is multi-axial: a device may belong to a product class, a functional class, a form factor, and a technology generation simultaneously. Do not force all classification into one exclusive tree.

## A. Mobile and personal communications
- Mobile radio and early cellular terminals
- 1G analog cellular phones
- 2G GSM, D-AMPS, cdmaOne and other digital cellular devices
- 2.5G/2.75G GPRS and EDGE devices
- 3G UMTS/WCDMA, CDMA2000 and related devices
- 4G LTE and LTE-Advanced devices
- 5G NR devices
- Feature phones
- Smartphones by operating-system ecosystem
- Satellite phones and satellite messaging devices
- PDA phones, communicators and hybrid handhelds
- Rugged, enterprise, push-to-talk and specialist handsets

Form factors: candybar, bar, flip/clamshell, slider, swivel, QWERTY, touchscreen slab, foldable, dual-screen, rollable, wearable/handheld hybrid.

## B. Computing systems
- Laptops: notebook, netbook, ultrabook, gaming, mobile workstation
- Convertibles: 2-in-1, detachable, tablet PC
- Desktops: tower, mini tower, SFF, USFF, all-in-one, mini PC
- Workstations: desktop and mobile
- Servers: tower, rack, blade, modular
- Thin clients and zero clients
- Legacy and historical computers
- Handheld computers and palmtops
- Single-board computers and compute modules

## C. Computer components and modules
- Mainboards, motherboards, daughterboards, backplanes and expansion boards
- CPUs, GPUs, NPUs, DSPs, SoCs, ASICs, FPGAs, CPLDs, MCUs
- RAM, ROM, SRAM, DRAM, DDR, LPDDR, GDDR, NAND and NOR
- HDD, SSD, NVMe, eMMC, UFS, optical and removable storage
- Chipsets, bridges, controllers and clock generators
- Power supplies, VRMs, PMICs, BMS and DC-DC converters
- Cooling assemblies, fans, heat pipes and vapor chambers
- Display panels, controllers, touch digitizers and backlights
- Wireless modules, modems, RF front ends, antennas and GNSS
- Connectors, cables, ports and interface modules

## D. Tablets, e-readers and displays
- Android, iPadOS, Windows and Linux tablets
- E-ink readers and writing tablets
- Portable monitors, smart displays and interactive panels
- Digital signage and professional displays

## E. Wearables and personal devices
- Smartwatches, fitness bands, smart rings
- AR/VR/MR headsets and spatial computers
- Smart glasses, body cameras and personal trackers
- Medical/wellness consumer electronics (device facts only; sensitive personal data excluded)

## F. Audio, imaging and entertainment
- Headphones, earbuds, speakers, microphones
- DACs, audio interfaces, amplifiers and mixers
- Cameras: compact, DSLR, mirrorless, action, cinema, surveillance, thermal
- Lenses, flashes, gimbals and capture modules
- Game consoles, handheld consoles, controllers and VR peripherals
- Media players, streaming devices, TVs and set-top boxes

## G. Connectivity and network infrastructure
- Cellular modems, hotspots, routers, gateways
- Access points, switches, bridges, repeaters
- ONT/ONU, optical transceivers and fiber equipment
- Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, UWB, Zigbee, Thread and LPWAN equipment
- Telecom base-station and radio access equipment

## H. Embedded, IoT and robotics
- Arduino, ESP, STM32 and other development boards
- SBCs, compute modules and industrial controllers
- Sensors, actuators, motor drivers and robotics controllers
- Smart-home hubs, locks, cameras and appliances
- Industrial IoT gateways and edge computers

## I. Automotive, industrial and scientific
- ECUs, infotainment, telematics, ADAS modules and vehicle sensors
- PLCs, HMIs, drives, CNC controllers and industrial networks
- Oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, spectrum analyzers, meters and lab instruments
- Test fixtures, calibration equipment and measurement systems

## J. Power and energy
- Batteries, cells, packs and battery management
- Chargers, adapters, wireless charging systems
- UPS, inverters, power stations and solar controllers
- Power distribution and protection modules

## Cross-cutting classification dimensions
- Historical era and release lifecycle
- Geographic market and regulatory region
- Form factor and physical construction
- Network/radio generation and supported bands
- Operating system, firmware family and boot architecture
- Processor architecture and semiconductor process (when sourced)
- Repairability and serviceability
- Security architecture and update lifecycle
- Environmental ratings and certifications
- Open-source support and community development status

## Historical mobile eras (non-exclusive)
- Pre-commercial mobile radio and cellular research
- 1G analog cellular
- 2G digital cellular
- 2.5G/2.75G transition
- 3G mobile broadband
- 4G LTE
- 5G NR and emerging post-5G research

Use actual launch/release evidence for each model; do not infer generation solely from its year.
