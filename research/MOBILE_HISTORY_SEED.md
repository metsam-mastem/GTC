# Mobile Phone History — Initial Sourced Baseline

**Status:** Initial research baseline, not a complete model catalogue.  
**Scope:** Major milestones from vehicle/mobile radio to cellular handhelds and network generations.

## Important distinction
"First mobile phone" can mean vehicle-mounted radiotelephone, portable cellular prototype, first commercial handheld handset, first GSM handset, or an early smartphone. GTC must preserve these definitions rather than force one ambiguous winner.

## Research cautions
- Distinguish prototype demonstration, regulatory approval, commercial launch, first shipment and retail availability.
- Distinguish first GSM handset from first GSM network and first SMS.
- Avoid calling a product "first smartphone" without defining the criteria; several early phone/PDA hybrids qualify under different definitions.
- Network-generation dates vary by country and operator. Store geography and event type.
- Historical milestone lists are not substitutes for a complete per-model registry.
